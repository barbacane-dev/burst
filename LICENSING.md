# Burst Licensing

Burst is dual-licensed. You may use it under the terms of either license, depending on your use case.

## GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3)

The default license. The full text is in the [LICENSE](LICENSE) file.

The AGPLv3 is suitable if you:

- Use Burst internally within your organisation
- Run Burst as a public service and make all source modifications available under the AGPLv3
- Build open-source software with a compatible license

As a copyleft license, if you run a modified version of Burst over a network, you must make your modifications available to users under the same license.

## Commercial License

A commercial license is available for organisations that cannot or do not wish to comply with AGPLv3 requirements.

A commercial license is necessary if you:

- Offer Burst as a hosted service without publishing source modifications
- Have business requirements that conflict with AGPLv3 copyleft obligations

The commercial license is **free** for:

- Small startups (annual revenue ≤ €1M and ≤ 10 employees)
- Nonprofits and academic institutions
- Open-source projects with open-source outputs

Larger organisations require a paid commercial license. See [barbacane.dev/pricing](https://barbacane.dev/pricing) or contact [contact@barbacane.dev](mailto:contact@barbacane.dev).

## Cryptographic Dependencies

Burst's cryptographic dependencies are covered by an OpenSSL exception within the AGPLv3 license, permitting the use and distribution of resulting binaries.
